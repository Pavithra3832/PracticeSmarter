About PracticeSmarter

Turn Practice Into Progress.

PracticeSmarter is an AI-powered interview preparation platform designed to help students prepare for technical and HR interviews in a simple, structured, and engaging way.

From realistic practice sessions and intelligent feedback to performance tracking and personalized improvement, PracticeSmarter brings essential interview preparation tools together in one platform.

Our Goal: Help students practice consistently, identify the areas they need to improve, and build the confidence to face interviews more effectively.
